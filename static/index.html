How Track Unification Works

When vehicles pass behind obstacles (such as light poles, signboards, or other passing vehicles), raw multi-object trackers assign a new track ID. Our Vehicle Identity Layer tracks spatial overlap, trajectory momentum, and visual license plate continuity to heal broken tracks and prevent duplicate counts.

Pre-OCR Quality Filters Applied

  • Motion Blur Gating: Drops frames with excessive vehicle motion blur (prevents garbage reads on fast moving vehicles).
  • Indian Plate Aspect Ratio Check: Rejects crops outside standard 1.8 – 6.0 width-to-height ratio.
  • Camera Watermark Masking: Ignores top/bottom CCTV overlay banners to avoid reading clock digits as plates.
  • Minimum Resolution Gate: Drops crops smaller than 40x15 pixels.
